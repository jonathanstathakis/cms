## Pairings

- high acid
- high tannin
- high alcohol
- highly aromatic
- pair with heavy dishes with high fat content, lard or butter
- need an acid component
- needs an acid - meat or cheese
- examples:
  - beef stew
  - truffle risotto
  - brisket mushroom pie
  - offal
  - gnocchi with gorgonzola cream cheese
  - roast duck
  - wild boar
- logic:
  - ripe red fruits and florals complement gamey savory dishes, high acid, alcohol, and tannin helping to cut through high fat
