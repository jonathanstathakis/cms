## Chianti

### Sight

- Light ruby
- garnet tones
- medium- -> medium conc.

### Taste

- fruit:
  - tart red fruit:
    - cherry
    - raspberry
    - cranberry
    - redcurrent
  - ripe orchard fruit:
    - red apple
    - black cherry
  - ripe berry:
    - mulberry
  - tomato
- floral:
  - dried flowers
  - potpourri
- herbal/green:
  - fennel
  - roast savory herbs
  - thyme
  - rosemary
  - majoram
  - camphor
  - bitter root (amaro?)
- spice:
  - clove
  - smoke
  - coffee/oak
- other:
  - blood
  - balsamic
  - animal
  - tar
  - leather
- mineral:
  - moderate to high minerality
  - clay
  - crushed rock
  - baked earth
- secondary:
  - oak:
    - options:
      - none
      - large neutral cask
      - mixed french oak barrique
    - Chianti DOCG maybe no oak
    - CC DOCG or CC DOCG Riserva oak

### Structure

- Chianti:
  - bone dry to dry
  - med -> med+ body
  - med+ -> high tannin
  - med+ -> high acidity
  - med -> med+ alcohol
