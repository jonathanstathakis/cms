#### Climatic Influences

- cold: sits between 48th and 49th parallels, mean annual temp of 10C. makes ripening unpredictable. The response is to create multi-year blends to achieve consistency [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].
- Influenced by the Atlantic Ocean. [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].
- frost, rain, fungal disease and hail are prominent hazards. Rain can interrupt flowering resulting in a *bouvreux* ('second crop') that does not ripen [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].

#### Soils

- belemnite chalk:
	- the soil consists of porous, belemnite chalk subsoil which absorbs heat to protect vines at night and provides great drainage - important in the wet climate [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].
	- belemnite chalk has a igh limestone content which allows vine roots to dig deeply [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].
	- There is a correlation between acidity and belemnite soils. [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].
- Micraster chalk
	- named for an extinct sea urchin [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024]
	- common in Champagne valley vineyards [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].
- Cellars
	- chalk and limestone are easy to carve, hence there is a multitude of cellars cut into these subsoils [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].
- Clay and sand covers much of the chalk [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].
- Aube
	- clay is the dominant soil type [@guildsomm_champagnesparklingwine_2024].

#### Labelling Terms

- Négociant Manipulant (NM): house that buys fruit from growers and other smaller houses. Notable labels include: Moët et Chandln, Louis Roederer Veuve Cliquot Ponsardin, Billecart-Salmon, Lanson, Taittinger, Pol Roger, Perrier-Jouët, Mumm, and Laurent-Perrier.
- Récoltant Manipulant (RM): Grower-producer making wine from estate-grown fruit. To qualify, 95% of the fruit must originate from the labelled estate.
