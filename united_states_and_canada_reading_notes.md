## Intro

### 01 Climatic Influence

<https://www.guildsomm.com/research/expert_guides/w/expert-guides/2443/napa-valley#03>:

- Napa Climate defined by interaction between maritime influence and hot dry Central Valley.
- Napa hot
- fog:
- San Pablo Bay fog
  - Napa cooler on valley floor
  - cooler in the south
  - fog protects from hot sun throughout day
  - fog lessens further north into valley
  - rising above fog line (1,400 feet) removes effect

Understanding Wines: Explaining Style and Quality (WSET):

California:

- California Ocean Current very cold
- Mountains north of LA shield the majority of the state from its influence
- fog:
  - cold air flows in at night due to pressure differentials
  - cold air brings fog
  - fog lingers through the morning, taking time to burn off
  - the effect is to cool otherwise hot days
- rain:
  - lack of rain and water shortages during growing season
  - no autumn rain enables longer and increased ripening

Oregon:

- Wilamette Valley:
  - moderate climate
  - cooling breezes from Pacific Ocean

Washington State:

- Columbia Valley AVA in the rain shadow of Cascade Mountains
- dry, long daylight hours, even summer temperatures.

### 02 AVA Principles

- Alcohol:
  - must be labelled, +- 1.5%
  - wines between 7 - 14% can be simply labelled as table wine or light wine.
- must include term "contains sulfites" on the label
- must include a government health warning.
- estate bottled:
  - must declare name and address of the bottler
  - 100% of the fruit must come from land owned or controlled by the winery
  - all fruit must be from same AVA
