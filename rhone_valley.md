## Intro

### 01 Climatic Influences

- _Mistral_:
  - cold northerly winds from the Massif Central
  - can damage vines
  - winter and and spring
  - does prevent disease by drying vineyard

#### Northern Rhône

- Moderate continental climate
- Rhône river
- river valleys that can protect from _Mistral_

#### Southern Rhône

- Mediterranean climate

#### Analysis

- As travel from north to south, vines move from Syrah and Viognier to Carignan and Rolle
- Assume that Grenache Noir dominant blends is the default wine for red, Grenache Blanc blends for white, with dominant grape dependent on microclimate.
- trying to identify trends in the white blends is difficult
- Chateauneuf-du-Pape refuses definition beyond restriction to the core set of grapes

#### AOPs: Northern Rhône

##### Côte-Rôtie AOP

- style:
  - rouge: Syrah with max 20% Viognier

##### Château-Grillet AOP

- style:
  - blanc 100% Viognier

##### Condrieu AOP

- style:
  - blanc: Viognier

##### Saint-Joseph AOP

- style:
  - blanc: Marsanne and Roussanne
  - rouge: Syrah blends

##### Crozes-Hermitage AOP

- style:
  - blanc: Marsanne and/or Roussanne
  - rouge: Syrah with max 15% Marsanne and/or Roussanne

##### Hermitage AOP

- blanc: Marsanne and/or Roussanne
- rouge: Syrah with 15% Marsanne and/or Roussanne
- vin de paille (blanc): Marsanne and/or Roussanne

##### Cornas AOP

- style:
  - Rouge: Syrah

##### Saint-Péray AOP

style:

- blanc: Marsanne and Roussanne
- blanc Mousseux Méthode Traditionnelle

##### Châtillon-en-Diois AOP

- style:
  - blanc: Chardonnay, Aligoté
  - rouge: Gamay blends
  - rosé: Gamay blends

##### Clairette de Die AOP

- style:
  - Mousseux blanc Methode Traditionnelle: Clairette
  - Mousseux blanc Methode Ancestrale: Muscat á Petits Grains, Clairette
  - Mousseux rosé Methode Ancestrale: Muscat á Petits Grains, Clairette, Gamay

##### Cremant de Die AOP

- style:
  - vin mousseux blanc: 55% Clairette, Aligoté, Muscat Blanc à Petits Grains

##### Coteaux de Die AOP

- style:
  - blanc: Clairette

##### Duché d'Uzèes AOP

##### Clairette de Bellegarde AOP

##### Costières de Nîmes AOP

- style:
  - blanc: Grenache Blanc, Marsanne, Rousanne blends

##### Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise AOP

- style:
  - Vin Doux Naturel Blanc
  - Vin Doux Naturel Rosé
  - Vin Doux Naturel Rouge

##### Rasteau AOP

- communes:
  - Rasteau
  - Cairanne
  - Sablet
- styles:
  - VDN:
    - ambré (tawny blanc):
      - Grenache Noir, Grenache Gris, Grenache Blanc
      - white wine with oxidative character
      - minimum 3 years aging before release
    - tuilé (tawny red):
      - Grenache Noir (min 75%), Grenache Gris, Grenache Blanc
      - red wine with oxidative character
      - minimum 3 years aging before release
    - blanc: Grenache Blanc, Grenache Gris
    - grenat:
      - Grenache Noir (min 75%), Grenache Gris, Grenache Blanc
    - Hors d'age:
      - 5 years maturation before release
    - rancio:
      - possesses oxidative characters (madeirized)
  - rouge: Grenache Blends

##### Lirac AOP

- style:
  - blanc: Bourboulenc/Grenache Blanc/Roussanne/Clairette blends
  - rosé: Grenache Noir, Cinsault, Syeah, Mourvèdre blends
  - Rouge: Grenache/Cinsault/Syrah/Mourvèdre blends

##### Tavel AOP

##### Luberon AOP

##### Vinsobres AOP

- style:
