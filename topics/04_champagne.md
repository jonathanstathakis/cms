## 01 Intro

### 01 Climatic Influences

- cold
- continental
- influenced by Atlantic Ocean
- hazards:
  - Frost
  - rain
  - hail
  - fungal disease
- Montagne de Reims:
  - upper slopes possess warm air allowing grapes to ripen

### 02 Champagne AC and Sub-Districts

- Champagne AOP:

  - styles:
    - Vin Mousseux Blanc
    - Vin Mousseux Rosé (saignée or blend)

  * Grapes:
    - PN, CH, Meu, PB, PG, Arb., Petit Meslier

- Sub-Districts:

  - Montagne de Reims (PN dom.)
  - Vallée de la Marne (PM dom.)
  - Côte des Blancs (CH dom.)
  - Côte de Sézanne (CH dom.)
  - Côte des Bar (Aube) (PN dom.)

  - Coteaux Champenois AOP:
    - red
    - white
    - rosé
  - Rosé de Riceys AOP:
    - 100% PN rosé
    - produced in Les Riceys (cru village in the Aube)

### 03 Permitted Grape Varietals

- Principal:
  - PN
  - CH
  - PM
- Auxillary:
  - PB
  - PG
  - Arb.
  - Petit Meslier

### 04 Soils

- General:
  - Belemnite chaulk subsoil (porous)
    - porous
    - high limestone content (deeper roots, higher acidity)
  - micraster chaulk:
    - present in the valleys
  - chalk:
    - easy to cut cellars.
    - generally covered by a thin layer of clay and sand.
- Aube:
  - clay dominant

### 06 Styles

Variables in style of wines in Champagne.

1. Sparkling:
   a. vintage or non-vintage
   b. encapagement:
   i. classic champagne cuvée
   ii. blanc de blanc
   iii. blanc de noir
   c. single or multi-vineyard
   d. extraction (blanc or rosé)
   e. sweetness levels: - brut nature: 0- 3 g/L, no added dosage - Extra Brut: 0 - 6 g/L - Brut: 0 - 12 g/L - Extra Dry: 12 - 17 g/L - Sec: 17 - 32 g/L - Demi-Sec: 32 - 50 g/L - Doux: >= 50 g/L
2. Still:
   a. red
   b. white
   c. rosé

### 08 Labelling Terms

TODO: check if there are other labeling terms.

1. Cru: Premier or Grand Cru
2. Champagne: wine is made in accordance with Champagne AOP. Note does not need to include "AOC/AOP" on the label
3. Matriculation Number. unique identifier for the wine:
   a. NM (_Négociant Manipulant_): classic négociant buying fruit from growers and vinifying undder their own labeling
   b. RM (_Récoltant Manipulant_): grower-producer, u.e. a Burgundian Domaine.
   c. CM (_Coopérative Manipulant_): growers coop making wine under a single brands
   d. RC (_Récoltant Coopérateur_): grower who has used a coop to vinify their fruit but sells the wine under their own label
   e. SR (Société de Récoltants\*): a group of growers making wine through a firm rarher than coop, and marketing wine under several brands
   f. ND (\_Nègocant Distributeur\*): the producer sells wine that it did not personally produce (?)
   g. MA (_Marque d'Acheteur_): A brand that the buyer itself commisioned, typically latge restaurnt chains or supermarkets. The wine is sold under the buyers label.
4. Special Club: Champagne producer belongs to the Special Club
5. NV/MV: multivintage blend.
6. encapagement:
   _ Blanc de Blancs: Chardonnay based wine
   _ Blanc de Noir: Pinot Noir/PM based wine
7. color: \* white or rosé
8. dryness level:

- Brut Nature: \* 0 - 3 g/L, no dosage
- Extra Brut: \* 0 - 6 g/L
- Brut: \* 0 - 12 g/L
- Extra dry: \* 12 -17 g/L
- Sec: \* 17 - 32 g/L
- Demi-sec: \* 32 - 50 g/L
- doux: \* >=50 g/L

9. VP: _Vieillissement Prolonge_
