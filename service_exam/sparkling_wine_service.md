# Sparkling Wine Service

A summary of sparkling wine service for CMS 2025 taken from [CMS Oceania](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Service-Standards-24.1.pdf):

1. Mise en Place:
   a. tray
   b. glassware
   c. bucket + stand with iced water to the neck of the bottle
   d. two service cloths
2. Order Taking:
   a. identify and greet host
   b. stand to the right of the host
   c. take the order - repeat back producer, vintage, producer, cuvée/vineyard.
3. Setting the Table:
   a. start with guest left of host and proceed clockwise.
   b. Glass top right of water glass.
   c. place cork coaster.
4. Presenting the Bottle:
   a. label up
   b. on service cloth
   c. repeat vintage, producer, cuvée / vineyard.
5. Opening Bottle:
   a. cut foil (keep label facing forward always)
   i. keep label facing forward
   ii. reach around with knife to cut foil on front face
   iii. turn knife around to cut back of face of foil.
   b. remove foil and place in bucket (cut diagonally to remove cleanly)
   c. grip bottle neck in left hand, place thumb over top of cork.
   d. loosen cork with right hand, keeping bottle pointing away from guest.
   e. untwist cage with 6 full turns and loosened.
   f. twist bottle away from cork, holding bottle at 45°.
   g. release cork without making noise
   h. remove cage from cork: place in pocket or present with cork
   i. wipe mouth of bottle with service cloth.
   j. place cork on coaster on table.
6. Pouring:
   a. single-stream pours: two if necessary. Complete each guest before continuing
   b. bottle in right hand, service cloth in left
   c. offer taste to the host
   d. step back, wait for approval
   e. pour ladies first proceeding in a clockwise rotation
   f. pour to 1/3rd.
   g. avoid drips: twist bottle at the end of the pour and wipe.
   h. finish with the host.
7. Conclusion:
   a. ask the host if can remove cork
   b. place bottle in ice bucket with clean service cloth draped over.
