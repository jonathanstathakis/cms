# Tête de Cuvée

A collection of descriptions of the Tête de Cuvée of top Champagne houses.

## Krug

### Grand Cuvee

- New edition every year
- blend of over 120 individual base wines
- blend of over 10 different vintages
- Brut sweetness ^[[Champagne 411](https://www.champagne411.com/krug/grand-cuvee.html)]

#### 127ème Édition

The following was taken from the [Krug](https://www.krug.com/en-int/champagne/krug-grande-cuvee-172eme-edition) website:

- 2016 harvest composes the core wine
- 146 wines
- 11 vintages: 1998 - 2016
- 42% reserve wine
- 44% PN, 36% CH, 20% Meu

#### Clos de Mesnil

The following was taken from [Krug](https://www.krug.com/en-int/champagne/krug-clos-du-mesnil-2008):

- The Clos du Mesnil is a vineyard in Champagne
- Located in Le Mesnil-sur-Oger (Côte des Blancs)
- Blanc de Blanc
- been made since 1979
- single site expression
- only made on special years
- hasnt' been made since 2008?
- pair with oysters, halibut, John Dory
